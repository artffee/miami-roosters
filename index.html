About the Book

This coloring book is our love letter to the city's most unbothered residents — flamingos in sunglasses, gators in Art Deco, and roosters who've clearly never met a stressful moment in their lives.

20–30 pages of Miami neighborhoods, landmarks, and legend — from Calle Ocho to Ocean Drive — for colorists of every skill level. Grab your gel pens, your markers, whatever — just don't grab the roosters. They bite.

Color loud. Live louder.

✨ 10% of net proceeds go straight to supporting the animals that make this city a little weirder and a lot more fun.
